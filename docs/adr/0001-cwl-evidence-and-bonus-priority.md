# CWL evidence rating and bonus priority

Status: accepted

Lineup recommendations use a 0–100 CWL evidence rating that combines current-CWL attack completion (60% when available) with observed regular-war attack performance (40% when available), renormalizing when one evidence source is missing. Regular-war history is not a regular-war lineup rule: signup owns regular-war opportunity, and the reusable activity gauge evaluates what a member did when they appeared in a war. It may support CWL review, larger-roster selection, follow-up, and promotion discussions. Bonus priority is separate from rotation: all members remain visible, members at eight or more CWL stars are ranked first by total CWL stars, and stars per war plus wars participated provide supporting context. The rotation recommendation may still use eight stars as a reason to rotate someone out. This preserves human judgment, recognizes contribution to the season result, and keeps unequal opportunities visible rather than hiding qualified members.

Regular-war member history is collected from current-war observations only. The official war log is not treated as a member-level backfill source, so a missing observation is shown as limited evidence rather than inferred.
