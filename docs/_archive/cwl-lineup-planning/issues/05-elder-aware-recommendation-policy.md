## Question

How should Elder status influence lineup recommendations, if at all, and how should that priority interact with availability, missed attacks, current-CWL reliability, rotation/eight-star goals, Town Hall fit, and explicit leader choices?

## Comments

- Provisional policy decision: availability/participation is the ultimate priority creator and eligibility gate. Verified Elder status is only a tie-breaker among otherwise comparable eligible members; it must not override availability, missed attacks, current-CWL reliability, rotation/eight-star goals, Town Hall fit, or explicit leader choices.

## Answer

Availability/participation is the primary eligibility and priority signal. A member must be eligible under availability before Elder status can matter. Verified current clan Elder status is applied only as a final tie-breaker among otherwise comparable eligible candidates. It cannot override unavailable or unknown availability, missed attacks, current-CWL reliability, rotation or eight-star goals, Town Hall fit, or an explicit leader choice.

If role data is missing, stale, or unrecognized, the Elder tie-breaker is not applied. When it affects a recommendation, the UI should explain that it was a tie-breaker rather than presenting Elder as a guarantee or score.
