# Promotion review uses six completed CWL attacks and missed assigned attacks as its signals

## Context
The clan's existing rule promotes to Elder after six completed CWL attacks. Leaders rotate members out, which lowers opportunity through no fault of the member. Alternatives: a composite score; automatic demotion on low participation.

## Decision
Six or more completed CWL attacks qualifies a member under the existing Elder rule. A member with fewer than six opportunities and no missed assigned attack is never automatically flagged for demotion. Being rotated out by leaders is neutral. Missing an assigned attack is a review/demotion signal. All promotion and demotion output is advisory for leader review.

## Consequences
- The post-CWL review surface ranks on evidence (0001) and lists candidates; it never changes a role.
- Regular-war absence is not a penalty (0001) — consistent with "opportunity is a leader choice" here.
