# CWL evidence rating and bonus priority

Lineup recommendations use a 0–100 CWL evidence rating based on observed current-CWL attack completion. Regular-war history is a separate activity/performance gauge: it evaluates what a member did when they appeared in a war, without treating signup-driven opportunity or absence as poor performance. It may support CWL review, larger-roster selection, follow-up, and promotion discussions, but it is exposed through evidence filters rather than silently folded into CWL lineup recommendations. Bonus priority is separate from rotation: all members remain visible, members at or above eight CWL stars are ranked first by total CWL stars, and stars per war plus wars participated provide supporting context. The rotation recommendation may still use eight stars as a reason to rotate someone out. This preserves human judgment, recognizes contribution to the season result, and keeps unequal opportunities visible rather than hiding qualified members.

Regular-war member history is collected from current-war observations only. The official war log is not treated as a member-level backfill source, so a missing observation is shown as limited evidence rather than inferred.
