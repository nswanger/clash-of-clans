Clan Muster identity

Three candidates for the mark, each drawn once on a 48×48 grid and then only scaled — so the 16px row is a real test rather than a redrawn shortcut. Gold is the constant; the mark is the only place the system is allowed a personality that is not neutral.

Decided: A everywhere, C at 16–32px

One mark with a container variant, not two marks — C is A knocked out of a shield by transform, so they cannot drift. The bare head wherever there is room; the badge where the container is what survives.

Illustration beyond the mark

None. There is no illustration style, no spot art, no mascot poses. The mark is one shape at one weight, recoloured but never redrawn — which is also why the empty state above reuses the mark at 56px rather than introducing a scene. A second illustration vocabulary would need a surface that demands it, and neither prototype produced one.
