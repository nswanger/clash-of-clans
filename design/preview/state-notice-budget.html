What changed, and why

Four of those seven were never notices. Two coverage gaps and one confidence note are per-recommendation explanations, hoisted out of the panel they explain and rendered as page alerts by a loop with no bound; they return to the change they describe. The rotation bar is an offer, not a problem, and belongs to the recommendation panel.

Two were chrome announcing nothing. “Latest saved lineup” is a bar that fires on the happy path. The documentation paragraph is onboarding copy mounted forever.

One was real — and it is the one still showing.

The rule

One region, highest severity first. When more than one notice qualifies, the region carries a count that expands the rest — so nothing is silently swallowed, but nothing stacks either. Only two things can ever occupy it: collection health, and a save conflict or failed request. Both are danger, both are page-scoped. Success, caution and info can never appear here at all.
