Recommendation

Decided: Archivo throughout. Chosen on a phone at true 1:1 size, which is the only way this question can honestly be answered — the 12px floor held up in Archivo, so the concern that it would be weaker than Inter at small sizes did not survive contact with the device.

One family rather than two: a single load, no pairing risk, and the broadcast-grotesque character the FotMob reference pointed at. Verified to ship tnum, so tabular figures work.
