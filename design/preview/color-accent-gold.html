Gold — a surface, not an ink

Gold is intrinsically light, so it behaves asymmetrically across themes. On a light surface real gold reaches only 2.30:1 — nowhere near the 4.5 text needs. Darkening it far enough (700, 5.37:1) turns it to bronze, which reads muted rather than clickable. So in light mode gold fills; it does not write. In dark mode it does both. That asymmetry is the system: one hue family, luminance shifting by theme, no second accent hue.
