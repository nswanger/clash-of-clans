Type scale

Today: 15 sizes, floor at 9px. 35 declarations sit at 11px or below and 18 at 10px or below — on the phone where you adjust lineups. Weights run to eight steps including 650 and 750, which are indistinguishable at these sizes.
Proposed: 7 steps, floor at 12px, 3 weights. The floor rises because legibility on a 390px screen is the point; 11px survives only for uppercase eyebrows, where letterforms are simpler and tracking opens them up.

Weights & tracking

Three weights: 400 body, 500 data emphasis, 600 headings and labels. Archivo adds 700 for display only. 650, 750 and 800 are dropped.
Tracking is bound to size, not chosen per use: negative above 20px, zero for body, +0.04em for uppercase labels only.
